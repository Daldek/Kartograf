   - Done 2026-11-09 ([ADR-061](decisions/0061-hsg-from-the-least-permeable-layer.md), branch `feat/hsg-depth-rule`, merged into `develop`; admitted during the freeze by the author's approval of the item's recommendations): `soil hsg --depth-rule least-permeable` (`calculate_hsg`/`check_hsg`/`validate_hsg_request(depth_rule=...)`, exactly one of `depth` and `depth_rule`, catalog `one_of` group `depth`) reads clay/sand/silt at 0-5, 5-15, 15-30, 30-60 and 60-100 cm, classifies each layer's USDA texture and takes per cell the layer with the lowest Ksat of its class by Rawls, Brakensiek & Saxton (1982) (`RAWLS_1982_KSAT_CM_H`; silt = silt loam); its group comes from `TEXTURE_TO_HSG` (ADR-025), whose order agrees with the Ksat order.
